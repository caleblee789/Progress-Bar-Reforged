See your progress. Plan your finish.

Progress Bar Reforged gives you a clear view of today's Anki study session: how much you have completed, how many cards are left, and when you are likely to finish.

The bar sits above or below your cards, keeping your progress in view while you study.

What's new in v1.1.7

  • Reviewer-focused gallery: the current release images show the reviewer in Light and Dark themes. Add-on behavior is unchanged from v1.1.6.
  • Redesigned Deck Breakdown: a clearer daily summary and larger deck workload text in Light and Dark themes.
  • Simpler controls: Sort and Hide empty sit with the table; the summary has no info or Copy buttons, and Sort choices fit without clipping.
  • Updated captures: the two images below show the reviewer in both themes, captured natively in Anki 26.9.3.

Make it yours

  • Simple or Advanced: keep just the essentials, or add review speed, time spent, time left, estimated finish time, and retention information.
  • A closer look at your decks: click the bar to see New, Learning, and Review cards by deck. Buried cards are listed separately from the cards available to study.
  • Your preferred layout: put the bar at the top or bottom, and show it during reviews only or also on the deck list and overview.
  • Light, Dark, or Auto: choose a theme or let the add-on follow Anki.
  • A shortcut when you need it: press Command+G on macOS or Ctrl+G on Windows/Linux to show or hide the bar. You can choose a different shortcut in Settings.

Get started

  1. In Anki desktop, open Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons.
  2. Enter 1511983907, then restart Anki.
  3. Start reviewing. The default is Advanced mode, shown during reviews only.
  4. To customize it, open Caleb M. Add-ons Settings → Progress Bar settings.

Already installed? Open Tools → Add-ons → Check for Updates, install the update, and restart Anki. Your saved preferences are kept.

Requires Anki desktop 2.1.49 or later.

Understanding your progress

The percentage compares answers completed with answers completed plus cards left. Answering the same card again counts as another answer. The filled area also adjusts for how often you have pressed Again recently, so it can differ from the percentage.

Finish times are estimates based on your recorded study pace. If there is not enough usable history yet, keep studying and an estimate will appear as you answer cards.
